# Automatic package policy

Existing bundled apps remain firmware-managed. They are preserved in the
cumulative image and are not reinstalled as dynamic packages. Automatic discovery
preserves externally added/user apps and performs no automatic removal.

The existing published policy remains unchanged. The source-only opt-in T023
bridge composes complete preserving next-boot core profiles; its new Runtime,
System, feed and product artifacts require exact admission before activation.
See `docs/t023/AUTOMATIC_CORE_BRIDGE.md` for required inputs and current blockers.

T-Watch remains internal-only. Its core profiles contain no SD mirrors or
fabricated removable-storage authority.
